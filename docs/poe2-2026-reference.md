## 2. 0.5.5 facts used on the site (thread 4000864)

- Expeditions are a core Atlas mechanic, including in Standard; they can be found from Act 4 onwards. The Verisium Anvil unlocks via The Runeseeker quest in Act 4.
- Sacred Blooms are a Ritual reward that can add the Viridian Wildwood to a cluster of Atlas Maps. They can only be found in Forbidden Rites. The Viridian Wildwood Unique Map is disabled.
- Trial of Chaos: a reward chest ends each room; completing a run rewards only Currency and Soul Cores; corrupted items are no longer a reward.
- A Grand Mirror's Delirium Fog spreads to 10 Maps, down from 8-14 (lower-Tier Maps) and 10-16 (high-Tier Maps).
- Arbiter of Ash: Fire Resistance and Cold Vulnerability; Arbiter of Divinity: Lightning Resistance and Fire Vulnerability (both replace All Elemental Resistances).
- 17 new Soul Cores (13 Jiquani, 4 Atziri). Atmohua's, Cholotl's and Zantipi's Soul Cores convert 40% of requirements (was 20%).
- No skill or support gem balance changes are listed in 0.5.5 to 0.5.5e.
